Partner Deliverables Checker

A prototype for organizing partner requirements and reviewing submission packages.

View source code ↗Demo & visual overview ↓

The problem

During CS Expo media-partnership coordination, requirements, captions, logos and deadlines can be spread across documents and conversations. It becomes easy to miss an item or follow an outdated instruction.

Give an event team one place to record approved requirements and review a submission against them.

Scope from the project brief

These are the intended capabilities. Implementation details will be confirmed during the local walkthrough.

Approved requirements

Record partner requirements and their source references.

Submission review

Compare a package with the approved requirements for its submission stage.

Actionable findings

Explain which deliverables appear missing or incorrect so a person can review them.

A decision to examine

A finding is useful only when the reviewer can understand the requirement behind it. Source references and an approval step are part of the intended workflow; the walkthrough will verify how the current prototype represents them.

Limitations

Checking a package does not establish that a partner has accepted it. Ambiguous instructions and final submission approval still need human review.
