My starting point

I’m a fourth-year Computer Science student specializing in Software Engineering at FEU Institute of Technology.

Coordinating CS Expo media partnerships gave me practical problems to explore: instructions spread across conversations, submission requirements that are easy to miss, and documents that need review before sharing.

That experience shaped Partner Deliverables Checker and ShareGuard. AccessProof explores a related concern in applications: whether people can access only what they are supposed to.

What interests me

My main direction is software development. I’m also interested in application security, privacy engineering and DevOps, particularly where testing and clear rules help make an application more dependable.
